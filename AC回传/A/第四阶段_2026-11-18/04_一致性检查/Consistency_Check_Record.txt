1. Four experiments and their scope

The 10 November formal experiment comprises two full 16-component runs on the same host with compensation disabled and enabled. Both runs reached 96 motion targets with 16 releases, 64 direct segments, 32 vertical sampled-IK segments, zero RRT executions and zero recorded collisions outside permitted contact. The maximum 3D position and orientation errors were 0.935791 mm and 0.155598 degrees with compensation disabled, and 0.261732 mm and 0.186519 degrees with compensation enabled.

C published a separate successful 16-component Windows reproduction on 15 November. It reached 96 targets in 607.1833 s and recorded maximum errors of 0.350763 mm and 0.586786 degrees. Its two videos and 12-check independent audit belong to this run.

On 17 November B restarted the recommended configuration on macOS ARM64 using the public entry point. All six feedback calibration tests passed. One representative block traversed six targets (four direct and two vertical), with one release, 11,300 joint samples at 240 Hz, 47.0833 s of simulated time and 236 video frames at 5 fps. C's audit utility recorded 94 inventory observations, and the scene retained all 19 bodies. Maximum final errors were 0.039958 mm and 0.00097745 degrees. The applied XY correction was 0.020105 mm. Its vector and the first component's immediate post-settle error each matched the corresponding 10 November enabled-run measurement to the published precision. Run_Summary_Recomputed.csv lists each run separately.

2. Placement decomposition and torque

For all 16 components in both formal runs, subtracting the target from the settled object origin reproduced the published signed world-axis offsets. The horizontal maximum decreased from 0.926721 to 0.227289 mm (75.474%), and horizontal RMS from 0.411093 to 0.104835 mm (74.499%). The signed mean vertical offset of the lower/upper layer was +0.039943/+0.129918 mm with compensation disabled and +0.039939/+0.129902 mm with compensation enabled. Both conditions used a 50 micrometre collision margin. Isolating its influence would require an experiment varying the margin.

The maximum orientation error increased by 0.030921 degrees. The enabled run's highest sampled equivalent torque was 15.1964 N m on J1 during transfer of component 11. The J6 full-run RMS rose by 2.271%. Reported equivalent torque is four times raw motor feedback, calibrated for the final internal substep observed at each external 240 Hz sample. The loaded robot and gripper mass is 4.67 kg, including a 1.02 kg fixed base. Torque combines gravitational, inertial and contact effects.
