CAD delivery for CIVL7023 Project 3
Part A fourth-stage package, 18 November 2026

01_Component contains the native AutoCAD model, editable FreeCAD model, STEP, and STL in millimetres and metres.
02_Assembly contains the 16-part two-layer octagonal CAD assembly and dimensioned 3D drawing. Native CAD and STEP use millimetres.
03_Engineering_Drawing contains the English A3 drawing as DWG, DXF, and PNG. Print the sheet at 1:1 on A3 to preserve each labelled view scale.

The component geometric origin is the bottom-face centre. Local X follows length, Y width, Z upward. The single-part bounding box is 50 x 30 x 25 mm. The layer pitch is 22 mm and the full assembly bounding box is 182 x 182 x 47 mm. Refer to the pose CSV for robot-world placements.

STL carries no unit metadata. Use Component_V2_m.stl for metre-based simulation and Component_V2_mm.stl for millimetre-based CAD. The approved mesh scale and origin are essential.

Formal drawings use English labels. The assembly shape and component interfaces match the frozen B/C simulation geometry. The candidate density remains 2500 kg/m3. Source filenames and repository SHA256 are recorded in the consistency-check directory.
